# TLDP Case Studies

The case library of Baruch's Technology Leadership Development Program, 2026–27 cohort.
Every student contributes at least one case by spring. Each case is a short, honest write-up of a
real problem worked with real data or a real scenario: what the situation was, what you did, what
happened, and what you'd do next.

Why it exists: a merged case here is public, reviewed work you can link from a resume and talk
about in an interview. It's also how the cohort learns from each other.

## Contribute a case

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Short version: copy the template, fill it in, open a pull
request. A staff member reviews it; when it merges, the bot announces it in the TLDP Discord with
your name.

## Where the data comes from

Cases use public data and public scenarios only: Tidy Tuesday, Open Case Studies, Kaggle, NYC Open
Data, published incident reports, your own hackathon or capstone work. Never include private data,
credentials, or anything under NDA. See CONTRIBUTING.md for the checklist.
